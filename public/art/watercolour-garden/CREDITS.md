## Notes for maintainers

- Keep this file next to the assets so a licence audit can trace every file.
- Do not add assets with unclear provenance (Pinterest, PNGTree, HiClipart,
  image-search results). Prefer CC0 museum collections or a platform with an
  explicit commercial licence.
- The manifest and blur placeholders live in
  `src/components/invitation/art.ts`; composition guidance is in
  [docs/ART_KIT.md](../../../docs/ART_KIT.md).
